# Provenance and limits

An honest account of where this tool's content came from, for anyone deciding
whether to use or adapt it. Last updated 2026-11-02.

## Status

This is not a validated instrument. No response data has been collected or
analysed, no factor analysis has been run, and it has not been compared with any
other instrument. The author's only check was taking it several times and seeing
that it roughly matched a DISC result from an earlier assessment.

## How the items were written

The project began after a leadership course and was first coded by hand. Most of
the questions, and later most of the code, were written by AI models from the
author's general knowledge of DISC and similar tests, then read and checked by the
author. They were not derived from published research or adapted from a specific
questionnaire. Prompts and model versions weren't recorded for the earlier items.

## DISC

- The DISC model goes back to William Marston's work. This library is not
  affiliated with any commercial DISC product.
- The library has 320 items, 80 per style: 45 worded forward and 35 reversed. It
  merges and hand-curates two earlier AI-written pools, plus 42 new reverse-worded
  items. Compound, jargon-heavy and confusingly negated items were removed.
- Each item counts toward its own style only. Assigning an item to a style is a
  judgement that hasn't been tested on real responses, and some wording may still
  reflect more than one style. Only factor analysis of real data can show that.
- A person's draw is 10 items per style, half reversed, and never both members of
  a mirrored pair.
- The app's response-quality checks work within one person. They don't replace
  sample-based reliability, and their thresholds were set by simulation.

## Working strengths

- The 24 strengths and three domains (Doing, Thinking, People) were written for
  this project and are not a mapping of any commercial framework.
- Each strength's DISC lean is a hand-set judgement used for one report section.
- The module is forced choice, so it ranks strengths within one person. It
  shouldn't be used to compare people with each other.
- In simulation, the standard length finds about 60% of a person's true top six and
  the deep length about 68%, so results are best read as clusters.

## Motivators and pressure modes

The eight motivators and four pressure modes were chosen from general knowledge,
not from a formal framework. Motivators are also forced choice, with the same limit
on comparing people.

## Reuse

The MIT licence covers what the author can license. Nobody has systematically
checked the AI-written text against commercial questionnaires, and copyright in
AI-generated text is unsettled and varies by country. This note is not legal advice.
